clonk

A personal assistant on iPhone. You talk to it like a friend who's good at things, and it gets on with the admin.

clonk answers questions, keeps track of the things in your life that tend to bite if forgotten (renewals, bookings, deadlines) and speaks up when one is coming. It can search your email and calendar when a task needs them, use a browser on its own computer, and draft or send things once you've said yes.

You connect the accounts you want it to use, and you can disconnect them at any time. Anything that sends, books or pays asks you first.

clonk is built and run by Jordan Rippon. It's a personal project and isn't available to the public yet.
